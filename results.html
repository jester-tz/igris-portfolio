Case studies

Blurberry Energy
Beverage brand · studio product shot & ad concept
Objective

Give a new energy drink a scroll-stopping hero image without booking an actual studio shoot.

Creative approach

Composited studio lighting, splash and condensation effects, and bold color grading tuned to the can.

Tools used

AI product visuals, manual compositing and retouching.

Final result

A campaign-ready hero shot delivered in days instead of weeks, at a fraction of studio cost.

Velora
Apparel e-commerce · product page
Objective

Design a product page that sells one hoodie clearly, with no distractions from the buy decision.

Creative approach

Large gallery imagery, an honest fabric close-up, and size/color pickers placed right next to the price.

Tools used

Shopify build, product photography direction, UI design.

Final result

A clean, confidence-building product page with related items surfaced right below the fold.
