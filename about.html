How I work

Less decoration.
More intention.

I started from visual work and gradually moved deeper into websites and digital products. These days I enjoy moving between the disciplines rather than forcing every job into one box.

That means I can think about the thumbnail, the edit, the landing page and the interaction as parts of the same story when a project needs it.

01

Start with the job

What should the piece make someone understand, feel or do?

02

Make the hierarchy obvious

Good design doesn't make people work to find the important part.

03

Finish the details

Spacing, type, states and motion are part of the work — not polish added at the end.

I want the work to feel like it came from a person with a point of view.
